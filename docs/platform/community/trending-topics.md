# Latest topics

The **Latest Topics** card lives in the right column of your dashboard. It lists the newest threads in the **[forum](../forum/overview)**, regardless of which workspace you're in.

## What it shows

The card lists the **three most recent** threads, newest first. Each one shows:

- **Topic title**: clickable, opens the thread.
- **Author name**.
- **Category**: e.g. *News & Announcements*, *OpenPLC - General Discussion*.
- **Comment count and view count**: small icons with numbers.

A **View Forum** button at the bottom takes you to the **[forum home](../forum/overview)**.

The list is global. There's no per-workspace list: whether you're on your personal dashboard or an org dashboard, you see the same topics.

## Why this widget is here

The forum is where most of the community help, bug reports, and discoveries happen. Surfacing the latest topics on the dashboard means new users find the conversation quickly without having to make the forum part of their daily check.

If you spend a lot of time in the forum, you probably want to bookmark the [Forum home](https://edge.autonomylogic.com/forum) directly instead of relying on the widget.
